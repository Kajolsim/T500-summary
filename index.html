Channel Context

Talent500 is a webinar-led inbound channel targeting mid-to-senior tech professionals (5–20 yrs exp). Funnel: webinar registration → attendance → sales call → enrollment. July delivered the best sales (53) and highest CVR (2.84%), confirming the channel's growth trajectory.

Key Insight

July delivered 53 sales — the highest ever, up 23% from June (43). The channel has shown an accelerating trajectory: Mar-Apr averaged 23.5 sales, while Jun-Jul averages 48 — a 2× improvement in monthly sales velocity. At the current pace, the channel is on track for ~₹7.1 Cr annualized revenue.
