Channel Context

Talent500 is a webinar-led inbound channel targeting mid-to-senior tech professionals (5–20 yrs exp). Funnel: webinar registration → attendance → sales call → enrollment. July was the best month across all metrics — leads, sales, and revenue — confirming the channel's growth trajectory. *Jul revenue estimated at avg ticket ₹1.60L.
